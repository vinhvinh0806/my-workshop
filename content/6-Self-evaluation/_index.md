### Needs Improvement

* **Improve discipline and time management:** I need to develop better habits for organizing my work, planning tasks in advance, and managing my time effectively to ensure that tasks are completed on schedule, especially when handling multiple responsibilities at the same time.

* **Improve problem-solving skills:** When facing new or complex problems, I sometimes need considerable time to identify the cause and determine an appropriate solution. In the future, I need to practice analyzing problems step by step, identifying root causes, and proactively trying different approaches before seeking assistance.

* **Improve communication skills:** I need to become more confident when expressing my thoughts, asking questions, and sharing ideas during the working process. I also want to improve the way I communicate so that my ideas are presented more clearly, concisely, and effectively when interacting with mentors and team members.

* **Strengthen technical knowledge:** I recognize that AWS and cloud computing cover a broad range of knowledge and technologies. Therefore, I need to continue studying and practicing to gain a deeper understanding of AWS services and how they are applied in real-world systems.

* **Be more proactive in seeking learning opportunities:** In the future, I would like to participate more actively in practical activities, communicate with experienced individuals, and explore new technologies to broaden my knowledge and gain a clearer direction for my future career.

Overall, I would rate my internship performance as **Fair**. I have made noticeable progress in technical knowledge, self-learning ability, sense of responsibility, and teamwork skills in a practical environment. However, I still have several areas that need improvement, particularly time management, problem-solving, and communication skills. I consider these areas important goals for my continued personal development and preparation for my future professional career after graduation.
