# Summary Report: "Saturday Meet up"

### Event Objectives

- Participate in hands-on activities and experience the working culture directly at the AWS Vietnam office.
- Learn about large-scale system design mindsets and career development paths within the AWS Cloud ecosystem.
- Understand the true essence of DevOps culture and the actual role of a Data Analytics Engineer in modern enterprises.
- Expand perspectives on multinational working environments and learn how to build a strong portfolio for upcoming internship and job opportunities.

### Speakers

- **Kien Nguyen & Tho Le** – Technical Specialists / System Architects
- **Nghi Danh Hoang Hieu** – AWS Partner Network / Former FCAJ Student
- **Trong H. Truong** – Senior DevOps Engineer
- **Dat Pham & Cuong Nguyen** – Data Analytics Engineers

---

### Key Highlights

#### 1. System Design: From Small Problems to High-Scale Architecture
- **URL Shortener Case Study:** Through the URL Shortener example, speakers emphasized that no feature is simple when placed in a high-scale environment.
- **Core Requirements:** A stable system must be carefully balanced regarding traffic volume, high availability, optimal performance, and cost efficiency.
- **Architectural Mindset:** Engineers must define the overall system architecture clearly before selecting any specific AWS service.

#### 2. Career Path: From First Cloud AI Journey to AWS Partner
- **Inspiring Story:** A growth journey from the first steps in the First Cloud AI Journey program to becoming part of the AWS Partner Network.
- **Key Takeaway:** Getting a job is just the starting point. An engineer's differentiation lies in a continuous self-learning spirit, active community contribution, and building a professional networking ecosystem.

#### 3. The True Essence of a DevOps Engineer
- **Dispelling Misconceptions:** DevOps is not merely about being proficient in Docker, Kubernetes, or configuring CI/CD pipelines.
- **Core Principle:** DevOps is a culture and set of practices for building, testing, and operating reliable systems built upon solid fundamentals in Linux, Networking, Git, Containers, and programming languages (Python/Golang).
- **Anti "Copy-Paste" Mindset:** Copying commands online does not mean you understand the root cause. A skilled DevOps engineer needs systems thinking and effective cross-departmental communication skills.

#### 4. Data Analytics Through a Multinational Lens
- **Actual Role:** A Data Analytics Engineer does not simply draw dashboards; the true value lies in critical thinking, root-cause analysis, anomaly detection, and "data storytelling" to guide business decisions.
- **Evolution Model:** Transitioning from *Observer/Learner* $\rightarrow$ *Problem Solver/Systems Thinker*.
- **Corporate Culture:** Exposure to a "Blameless post-mortem" culture and international recruitment standards.

---

### Key Takeaways

#### Mindset & Foundations
- **Foundations are Paramount:** Instead of blindly chasing tech trends, mastering core fundamentals (Networking, Linux OS) is the golden key.
- **Big Picture Perspective:** Cultivate the ability to view the entire infrastructure landscape rather than focusing solely on completing isolated assigned tasks.

#### Hands-on & Soft Skills
- **Learn by Doing:** Build personal projects (Pet Projects) to directly deploy, configure monitoring, face errors firsthand, and optimize systems.
- **Soft Skills as a Launchpad:** Presentation, communication, and teamwork skills play a crucial role in an engineer's career progression.

---

### Applying to Work & Personal Projects

Applying these key lessons directly to the current Marketplace system development project:
- **Architectural Clarity:** Map out and clarify the relationships between Frontend, Backend, Database, and Object Storage (Amazon S3).
- **Documentation & Security:** Establish a proper documentation workflow, enforce security policies, monitor operational costs, and standardize deployment pipelines.
- **Critical Thinking:** Move beyond making the system "just work" by answering: *Why choose this specific AWS service? What are the current architecture bottlenecks, and how can they be resolved?*

---

### Event Experience & Reflections

Participating in the event at the AWS Vietnam office provided invaluable insights into real-world corporate expectations for graduating students. Technical expertise, practical problem-solving skills, and a collaborative attitude must be cultivated side by side.
