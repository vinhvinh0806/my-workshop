During my participation in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program, I had the opportunity to explore cloud computing and the **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** ecosystem through self-study, learning materials, workshops, mentor discussions, and teamwork.

The program was carried out over approximately **12 weeks**, with the learning activities and assigned tasks organized into different stages. During the first few weeks, I focused on becoming familiar with AWS, understanding fundamental cloud computing concepts, and learning about basic AWS services. I then continued to explore services such as **AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM)** and **Amazon Virtual Private Cloud (Amazon VPC)**, while also researching cloud architecture and related technical concepts.

Throughout the program, I combined theoretical learning with practical research, participated in workshops, discussed ideas with mentors and team members, and gradually applied the knowledge I gained to the direction of the project.
