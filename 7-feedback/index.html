Sharing and Feedback

Here, you can freely share your personal opinions about your experience participating in the First Cloud AI Journey program. This will help the FCAJ team improve any areas that may need further development based on the following aspects:

Overall Evaluation

1. Working Environment
The working environment at FCAJ provides me with a comfortable atmosphere and plenty of opportunities to learn independently. I never feel too pressured when asking questions or discussing topics that I do not fully understand, as everyone is approachable and willing to share their experiences. What I appreciate most is the balance between a serious working attitude and a comfortable communication style, which has helped me adapt more easily and become more confident throughout my internship.

2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin
Throughout my internship, I received close support from both my mentor and the admin team. My mentor not only provided technical guidance but also helped me develop better ways of thinking and approaches to problem-solving. When I encountered a difficult topic, I was usually encouraged to research and experiment on my own first before receiving feedback and further guidance. This approach helped me become more proactive in learning and more confident when dealing with unexpected challenges. In addition, the admin team responded promptly to matters related to schedules, learning materials, and the overall program, allowing me to focus more effectively on my studies and assigned tasks.

3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major
The tasks I worked on during my internship helped me realize that the knowledge I learned at university can be applied to real-world situations more than I initially expected. In addition to applying fundamental knowledge in Information Technology, I also had the opportunity to explore new technologies and areas, especially AWS and cloud computing. This experience allowed me to review and strengthen my existing knowledge while expanding my understanding of technologies commonly used in professional environments. I believe this was a valuable experience that helped me gain a clearer perspective on my future professional development.

4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities
My internship experience significantly improved my ability to learn independently and take initiative in my work. I learned how to search for resources, evaluate and select relevant information, and persist in solving problems instead of relying entirely on guidance from others. Besides technical knowledge, participating in discussions and collaborating with others also helped me better understand teamwork and the responsibility each individual has toward shared goals. These experiences have made me more confident and given me a clearer understanding of the skills I need to continue developing for my future career.

5. Company Culture & Team Spirit
What impressed me during my internship was the strong spirit of collaboration among team members. Everyone has clearly defined responsibilities, but when problems arise, people actively communicate and support one another to find solutions. I also noticed an open and approachable communication style, which made me feel comfortable sharing my opinions and asking questions despite having limited experience. Over time, I gradually became more integrated into the team and gained a better understanding of the importance of responsibility, collaboration, and mutual support in a professional working environment.

6. Internship Policies / Benefits
I highly appreciate the practical support policies provided to interns, which helped me feel more comfortable and secure throughout the program. In addition to flexible support regarding working time, I also had opportunities to participate in internal training and knowledge-sharing activities. I found this particularly valuable because it not only helped me improve my technical knowledge but also demonstrated that the company genuinely creates opportunities for interns to learn and grow in a real-world working environment.

Additional Questions

  • What did you find most satisfying during your internship?
    The thing I am most satisfied with is having the opportunity to experience a real working environment and explore new areas of knowledge on my own. I especially appreciate the support from my mentor and everyone at FCAJ, as I always had someone to turn to when facing difficulties while still being encouraged to research and solve problems independently. Through the internship, I feel that I have improved in both technical knowledge and my ability to take initiative in my work.

  • What do you think the company should improve for future interns?
    Based on my personal experience, I feel that the current program already meets most of the essential needs of interns. If there is room for further improvement, I think the program could provide more time for practical activities or organize review sessions at different stages of the internship. This would give interns opportunities to reflect on what they have learned, discuss challenges they encountered, and receive additional guidance for their next steps. I believe this could make the overall internship experience more structured and meaningful.

  • If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?
    Yes. I would recommend FCAJ to my friends, especially those who are interested in learning more about AWS, cloud computing, and gaining experience in a professional working environment. I believe it is a suitable place for students to develop self-learning skills, improve their technical knowledge, and become familiar with professional working practices. In addition, the support from mentors and the open-minded atmosphere make it easier for interns to adapt and have a positive experience throughout the program.

Suggestions & Expectations

  • Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?
    I think the current internship experience is already quite comprehensive and suitable for students. If there is an opportunity for further improvement, I would like to see more opportunities for interns to interact and communicate directly with employees working in different roles within the company. Sessions where employees share their real-world work experiences, career development journeys, or professional insights would help interns gain a better understanding of different career paths in the industry and motivate them to define their own future direction.

  • Would you like to continue this program in the future?
    Yes. I sincerely hope that the FCAJ program will continue and develop further in the future. If given the opportunity, I would also like to participate in future learning activities or advanced programs to strengthen my knowledge, keep up with new technologies, and continue developing the skills I gained during my internship.

  • Any other comments (free sharing):
    I feel fortunate to have had the opportunity to participate in the FCAJ program and experience a real learning and working environment. This period not only helped me improve my technical knowledge but also gave me the opportunity to better understand myself, my learning process, and how I work as part of a team. I would like to sincerely thank the mentors, the admin team, and everyone at FCAJ for accompanying and supporting me throughout the program. I hope the program will continue to grow and provide meaningful experiences for many future generations of interns.
